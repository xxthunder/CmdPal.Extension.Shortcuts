# Privacy

Shortcuts for Command Palette reads only the folders you configure in its settings (by default `%USERPROFILE%\shortcuts`), to list the files in them. It writes there only when you run "create example folder".

It keeps its settings and the history of script arguments on your machine, in its own settings folder. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the extension collects no telemetry.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/xxthunder/CmdPal.Extension.Shortcuts/issues.
