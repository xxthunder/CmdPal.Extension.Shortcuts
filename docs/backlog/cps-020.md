# [CPS-020] The watcher's burst test does not fail on a slow CI runner

**Status**: In Progress
**Priority**: High
**Component**: `tests/FoldersCatalog.Tests/CatalogWatcherTests.cs`

**Summary**:
As the author, I want `develop` to turn red only when something is broken, so that a red CI
run means a defect and not a slow runner.

**Description**:
`CatalogWatcherTests.BurstOf20Files_CallsChangedOnce` writes 20 files with a real
`FileSystemWatcher` and a quiet time of 100 ms, and asserts exactly one call. On the shared
Windows runner a pause of more than 100 ms between two writes (Defender, IO load) splits the
burst in two, and the debouncer rightly calls twice: `Expected: 1, Actual: 2`. It failed on
`develop` on 2026-11-17, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-21 without a change to `src/` or `tests/`;
the runs in between were green.

The product is right, the test asserts a timing it cannot hold. That a burst leads to exactly
one call is already tested deterministically with a fake clock in
`DebouncerTests.Burst_RunsOnceAfterTheLastSignal`. What the watcher test can show on real time
is that the burst is reported and debounced at all.

**Scope Decisions**:
- **2026-11-21 - The burst bound is fewer than the files written**: the burst test asserts at
  least one call and fewer than 20. Without the debouncer, 20 writes give at least 20 calls
  (each write raises Created and Changed), so any count below 20 shows the burst was
  debounced; a tighter bound would bring back a dependency on the runner's speed.
- **2026-11-21 - Every watcher test asserts at least one call**: one `File.WriteAllText`
  raises Created and then Changed, so the single-file tests can split in two on a slow
  runner just like the burst. All tests that expect a call assert at least one, through one
  helper, and drop `Once` from their names. That a signal leads to exactly one call stays
  covered with the fake clock in `DebouncerTests`.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] `BurstOf20Files_*` asserts at least one call and far fewer calls than files written, not exactly one
- [ ] No other test of `CatalogWatcherTests` depends on the runner's timing for an exact call count, or the item names why it holds
- [ ] All existing tests continue to pass
